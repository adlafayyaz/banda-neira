Before you book.

How do I check boat schedules?

Check PELNI’s current timetable and confirm the sailing with your operator. Connections and weather can change; leave room in your travel plans.

Check current sailings
Can I visit neighboring islands?

Ask your accommodation or a local operator about boats to Pulau Banda and Pulau Run. Agree on the route, price, and return arrangement before setting out.

Do I need a ticket for the island?

There is no general island admission ticket. Individual attractions, transport, and activities may charge separately. Confirm fees at each place.
